# Kiosk Staff Guide

Changes you save in Admin show to visitors right away. Export a backup when you finish (section 5).

## 1. Getting into Admin

1. Tap the **top-left corner** of the screen **5 times quickly** (within 3 seconds). A keypad opens.
2. Enter the staff PIN and tap **Enter**.
3. Use the menu on the left: **Dashboard, Eras, Events, Media, Settings**.
4. When you're done, tap **Exit admin** (bottom of the menu).

- **Forgot the PIN?** Tap **Forgot the PIN?** on the keypad and enter the 12-digit recovery code. Then pick a new PIN and write down the new recovery code it shows.
- You're signed out after **10 minutes** without a touch. Unsaved changes are lost, so tap **Save** often.

## 2. Pictures and videos

A picture goes into the **Media** library first, then onto an event.

1. **Media → Add media**, then pick the file (from a USB drive or the kiosk's Downloads folder). Photos: JPEG, PNG or WebP. Videos: MP4, plus a captions file (.vtt) and a poster image.
2. Fill in **Alt text** (what the photo shows, for visitors who can't see it), **Caption** and **Credit** (like "NASA"). Tap **Add to library**.
3. Open **Events → Edit** on the event, tap **Add a photo or video** and choose it. An event holds up to **3**; the **first** is its main picture (use the move buttons to reorder). Tap **Save event**.

- To fix a caption or credit: **Media → Edit**.
- A photo still used by an event can't be deleted. Remove it from the event first.

## 3. Events and quiz questions

1. **Events** lists every event by era. Use **Search events** to find one, then tap **Edit**. For a new one, tap **Add an event**.
2. Fill in the **Title**, a **Short title** for the timeline (28 characters at most), the **Sort date** (like 1969-07-20, sets the order), the **Date to show** (like "1969"), **Status**, at least one **Era**, a **Summary** and the **Body** text.
3. Tap **Save event**, then **Preview** to see it as visitors will.

- **Hide without deleting:** turn off **Published** and save.
- **Quiz questions** are at the bottom of each event (save a new event first). Each question has 2 to 4 choices, one marked correct, and an explanation.
- A **Needs review** tag means a planned or ongoing event hasn't been checked in 90 days. Check the facts, then tap **Mark reviewed**.

## 4. Eras

1. **Eras** shows the timeline's sections in order. Use the move buttons to change the order.
2. Tap **Edit** (or **Add an era**), change the **Title**, **Subtitle**, **Overview**, **Start year** and **End year** (blank = Today), then **Save era**.

- The era's card photo is the first photo of its earliest event. To change it, change that event's photos.
- An era with events can't be deleted. Move its events to another era first.

## 5. Settings and backups

**Backups (do this after every round of changes):** **Dashboard → Export backup**. It saves one .zip file to the kiosk's **Documents/InfinityKiosk** folder. Copy it to a USB drive or the shared Drive's **Kiosk-Backups** folder, with the date in the name. **Import backup** replaces all content with a backup (the current version is kept first).

**Settings** holds the visitor timeout, the welcome screen video, the certificate website, **Change the staff PIN**, the **Recovery code**, and **Kiosk lock**. To install an app update, tap **Unlock for maintenance** first; restarting the kiosk locks it again. **Open Android settings** (under Kiosk lock) leaves the app for Android's Settings; it's there when the kiosk lock isn't set up, or after Unlock for maintenance.

**Keep the PIN and recovery code** somewhere safe, away from the kiosk. If both are lost, getting back in means erasing the kiosk.
